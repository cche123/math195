\title{Acoustic Characteristics and Emotional Mood in Electronic Dance Music}
\author{Caden Che and Kevin Zuniga}
\thanks{Exploratory analysis prepared for MATH 195, Stanford University.}
\begin{document}
\onehalfspacing
\begin{abstract}
We investigate which musical characteristics help explain the emotional mood of electronic dance music using 92 provisional dance-style candidates from MERGE Audio Complete. Seven interpretable acoustic features predict supplied valence and arousal scores through separate ridge regressions evaluated using nested artist-credit-group cross-validation. Arousal RMSE decreases from 0.0992 for a training-mean baseline to 0.0931, with out-of-fold $R^2=0.118$. Valence prediction does not improve over the baseline. Spectral brightness and spectral change provide the clearest contribution to arousal prediction. An exploratory temporal extension yields small, split-dependent changes in error. The results concern a provisional sample and static supplied labels; verified full-song section analysis and personal case studies remain future work.
\end{abstract}
